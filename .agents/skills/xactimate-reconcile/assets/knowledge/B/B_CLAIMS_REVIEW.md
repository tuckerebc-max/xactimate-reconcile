# AI review 1 — Claims scope, cost and evidence

**Date:** 2026-09-21. **Reviewer:** Codex AI, claims review lens. **Mode:** NON-INDEPENDENT, sequential review by the same AI that prepared the files. This is not review by a licensed adjuster, engineer or roofer.

**Materials reviewed:** B_RESEARCH.md; all 24 B_ARGUMENTS.json records; B_TEMPLATES.md; relevant source records. **Outcome:** Suitable as a conditional research/template packet; no actual claim or dollar request is validated.

| Challenge applied | Finding and disposition |
|---|---|
| Can a larger estimate or target recovery substitute for evidence? | No. Every issue requires facts/evidence and a concession condition; no recovery number supplied. |
| Can repair and replacement enter the same total? | The template prohibits adding mutually exclusive alternatives and requires a stable location/line crosswalk. |
| Does discontinued tile automatically support replacement? | No. Availability, salvage, compatibility and appearance are distinguished in B-I05 and B-I07. |
| Does a brittleness claim or flat-roof moisture observation prove full scope? | No. Methods, representativeness, alternative causes and least expansive feasible scope remain required. No technical threshold is invented. |
| Are waste, accessories, mitigation, markup and taxes added twice? | B-I10–B-I15 require inclusion checks and explicit bases. The template contains a duplicate-removal step. Actual costs remain unknown. |
| Can gross estimate change be presented as money owed? | No. The template separates gross scope from RCV/ACV, both depreciation categories, deductible, limits and payments. Unknown inputs block a payment conclusion. |
| Does every future requested correction connect to a physical observation, exhibit and dollars? | The discrepancy row requires line/page/location, condition, exact exhibit locator and calculation. No populated claim row exists; enforcement at live intake remains necessary. |
| Is there a material format problem? | The detailed schedule is wide Markdown, useful for structured handoff but will need landscape formatting or row-by-row presentation if later converted to a letter/PDF. No render is claimed. |

**Concrete refinements retained:** no automatic zero for an unlocated carrier allowance; requested and carrier quantities must share units/scope; appearance-only work remains separate; alternative scopes cannot both be counted; quote versus actual expense and payment reissues are explicit.

**Remaining claims inputs:** complete original estimates; roof inspection and condition evidence; causation record; actual product and availability; measurements; price components; local requirements; payment ledger. A competent human must verify technical conclusions before any actual submission.

**Failure conditions:** unsupported roof-wide extrapolation, missing source photographs, duplicate operation, unverified unit conversion, unrelated deterioration, absent policy evidence or an unexplained payment balance. Any of these requires revision or a visible hold on the affected request. The structural validator cannot detect all of them.
