# Licensed Xactimate operator playbook

This is an operator handoff, based on A's dated official-document research. Navigation describes the researched desktop/X1 workflows; verify the installed version and configuration. The application is not controlled by this harness and proprietary price data is not bundled.

| Step | Operator action | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preserve original carrier and contractor estimates, native projects and readable reports; establish comparable financial bases | Versions, dates, direct versus gross totals, inspection limits |
| 2 | Record project and checkpoint price lists in Claim Info > Parameters | Exact list IDs, month, geography and why that basis applies |
| 3 | Reconcile Sketch by face, slope, boundary, opening and relevant length | Measurement report, field readings and original/revised sketch |
| 4 | Inspect selected item detail and labor/material/equipment components | Selector/activity/unit, definition and inclusions from licensed software |
| 5 | Check macros, automatic waste, manual factors, starter and ridge | Settings and one nonduplicative takeoff |
| 6 | Add line notes and supporting documents/images; F9 is documented for line notes | Observation, location, exhibit, quantity derivation, inclusion check |
| 7 | Justify price overrides and check tax, O&P and minimum-group settings | Comparable quotes and explicit eligible bases; no default percentages |
| 8 | Run the configured software inspection | Resolved material findings and any justified exception; XactXpert only if available/activated |
| 9 | Save Variation and the separate scope ledger; export readable report/native ESX as authorized | Variation shows checkpoint pricing, not full semantic estimate reconciliation |
| 10 | Reconcile the revised export to the memo and address remaining physical questions | Exact amounts, difference log, operator identity and focused reinspection questions |

For every runtime issue, use the generated checklist to connect the carrier line to the proposed operation and expected direct delta. Verify equivalent units and component scope before entering changes. Do not bill repair and replacement of the same work or an all-inclusive quote plus its included components.

Use `operator-check` to preserve the actual exported PDF/ESX and compare the operator-declared direct subtotal. It cannot verify the declared subtotal independently or parse ESX. Confirm it visually against the export and record the method and remaining financial issues in the notes. The native estimate remains a separate deliverable.

Sources: A-S02 through A-S15 and A-S22 through A-S24 in `assets/knowledge/A/A_SOURCES.json`. Source capability is distinct from recommended operator control and claim-specific necessity.
