# Estimating and argument review cards

These are conditional review methods from research dated September 21, 2026. They do not establish a defect in any particular estimate, coverage, a local price, or a required addition. Start and finish with A-O20. Apply B-I01 causation, B-I23 role and B-I24 date/scope checks throughout.

## A-O01 Measured roof geometry and slopes

**Potential discrepancy:** Different surface area, face selection, pitch, overhang or length takeoffs.

**Calculation:** For a planar face only: plan area × sqrt(1+(rise/12)^2); sum verified faces. Delta = corrected quantity × matching rate minus existing allowance.

**Evidence required:** Face-labelled sketch; field slope readings; aerial report limitations; perimeter/opening measurements.

**Likely objection:** Carrier used a reliable measurement report.

**Supported reply:** Identify the specific face discrepancy and reproduce the measurement.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** No verified discrepancy; surface area already slope-adjusted.

**Overlap risk:** Applying slope factor twice; billing excluded/unaffected faces.

**Xactimate action:** Correct Sketch by face; retain original and measurement notes.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I02. **Sources:** A-S22, A-S03.

## A-O02 Waste, starter, ridge and accessory quantities

**Potential discrepancy:** Order quantity, layout loss or accessories differ from measured installation quantity.

**Calculation:** Derive layout/cut loss and product coverage; separate justified starter/ridge LF from field material; subtract existing included allowance.

**Evidence required:** Roof layout; package coverage; cuts/valleys; accessory specification; waste setting capture.

**Likely objection:** Waste or accessories already included.

**Supported reply:** Show a single auditable material takeoff and settings comparison.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Arbitrary percentage; ordering surplus unrelated to necessary work.

**Overlap risk:** Manual formula plus auto waste; field shingles for ridge plus separate cap.

**Xactimate action:** Audit manual/automatic waste and every accessory line.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I11. **Sources:** A-S24, A-S05.

## A-O03 Material identity, quality and assembly specification

**Potential discrepancy:** Carrier priced a different shingle grade, tile profile or low-slope assembly.

**Calculation:** Same supported quantity × verified like-kind unit-price difference; separate any elected upgrade.

**Evidence required:** Labels; samples; dimensions; product identification; invoices; manufacturer system literature.

**Likely objection:** Higher-price item is an upgrade.

**Supported reply:** Tie specification to existing material or applicable required replacement assembly.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Unidentified product; premium election; unsupported availability claim.

**Overlap risk:** Material allowance plus all-inclusive supplier/installer quote.

**Xactimate action:** Select item only after licensed detail confirms specification; attach identification.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I05, B-I10, B-I12. **Sources:** A-S03, A-S21, A-S20.

## A-O04 Repairability and replacement boundary

**Potential discrepancy:** Localized repair versus slope/system replacement extent.

**Calculation:** Price documented repair method and documented necessary replacement alternative separately; compare complete scopes.

**Evidence required:** Damage map; repair feasibility assessment; compatibility/availability evidence; condition; manufacturer guidance.

**Likely objection:** A durable localized repair is feasible.

**Supported reply:** Explain the specific failed repair premise and narrower alternatives considered.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Age, appearance or isolated damage alone; destructive test without authorization.

**Overlap risk:** Repair and replacement charged for same area; unrelated slopes.

**Xactimate action:** Use separate scenarios; replace overlapping allowance only after scope support.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I04, B-I05, B-I06, B-I07. **Sources:** A-S18, A-S20.

## A-O05 Tear-off, existing layers and substrate exposure

**Potential discrepancy:** Removal scope omits verified layers or assumes recover is suitable.

**Calculation:** Measured affected area × verified removal activity/layers; subtract base tear-off already included.

**Evidence required:** Edge/cut observations; tear-off photos; documented layers; selected assembly instructions.

**Likely objection:** Removal is included or recover is feasible.

**Supported reply:** Locate the extra operation and prove its layer/area.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Unverified layers; unnecessary removal; unrelated reroof upgrade.

**Overlap risk:** Composite removal plus separate same removal; removed layer counted twice.

**Xactimate action:** Inspect activity and component scope; distinguish base and additional removal.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I03, B-I06. **Sources:** A-S03, A-S04, A-S21.

## A-O06 Underlayment type, layers and integration

**Potential discrepancy:** Allowance differs from actual or applicable replacement underlayment system.

**Calculation:** Supported area and laps × matched assembly rate; isolate flashing/tie-in operations not included.

**Evidence required:** Product/age identification; roof slope; opened-area photos; applicable instructions/evaluation report; AHJ trigger if relied upon.

**Likely objection:** Existing material can remain or repair; premium underlayment is optional.

**Supported reply:** Demonstrate necessary removal/replacement and correct assembly at the documented slope.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Age-only maintenance; no observed damage or repair necessity; generic code claim.

**Overlap risk:** Full barrier and felt on same area without assembly basis; laps included twice.

**Xactimate action:** Use supported assembly and quantity; record included layers.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I10, B-I04. **Sources:** A-S19, A-S05.

## A-O07 Tile lift/reset, salvage and breakage

**Potential discrepancy:** Underlayment access labor, reusable tiles or evidenced unavoidable breakage omitted/misallocated.

**Calculation:** Lift/reset affected area plus documented replacement count; credit retained material and superseded repair work.

**Evidence required:** Access plan; tile inventory; fastening method; pre-work condition; observed breakage log; matched replacement supply.

**Likely objection:** Tiles can be locally repaired; breakage allowance is speculative.

**Supported reply:** Separate sound reusable tiles, pre-existing storm fractures and supported handling losses.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Blanket breakage rate; contractor-caused damage presented as storm damage; full replacement without proof.

**Overlap risk:** Full tile replacement plus lift/reset on same tiles; breakage counted as waste and replacements.

**Xactimate action:** Create distinct supported operations and contingent unresolved quantities.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I05, B-I11. **Sources:** A-S20, A-S19.

## A-O08 Low-slope membrane, coating and insulation assembly

**Potential discrepancy:** A coating/patch allowance differs from a demonstrably necessary membrane/assembly repair.

**Calculation:** Measure affected layers, field and upstands separately; compare complete warranted repair alternatives.

**Evidence required:** Identify membrane/coating/foam and substrate; product labels/history; qualified condition assessment; moisture investigation where indicated; adhesion/compatibility results; current applicable repair/restoration detail.

**Likely objection:** Coating or localized patch restores performance.

**Supported reply:** Show why the lesser repair cannot address the established damage and compatible repair requirements.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Routine maintenance/recoat alone; unidentified system; moisture without cause/extent evidence; an inapplicable commercial restoration specification.

**Overlap risk:** Complete system line plus base/cap/primer already included; roof and wall area overlap.

**Xactimate action:** Use system-specific detail; separate pending insulation/deck findings.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I06. **Sources:** A-S21, A-S03, A-S26, A-S27, A-S28, A-S29.

## A-O09 Drip edge, eave/rake metal and tile closures

**Potential discrepancy:** Perimeter metal or closures omitted, mismeasured or unnecessarily replaced.

**Calculation:** Verified affected LF by edge type × applicable operation; preserve reusable sections where appropriate.

**Evidence required:** Edge photos; LF takeoff; metal/closure profile; attachment and integration method.

**Likely objection:** Undamaged metal can be reused or is already priced.

**Supported reply:** Explain damaged section or inability to complete supported repair with existing component.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Blanket replacement without integration/condition evidence.

**Overlap risk:** Drip edge plus eave/rake line with same metal; accessory in composite scope.

**Xactimate action:** Separate edge types and operations; check inclusions.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I10, B-I08. **Sources:** A-S05, A-S19, A-S21.

## A-O10 Valleys, walls, chimneys, skylights and penetration flashings

**Potential discrepancy:** Counts, lengths, flashing stages or associated access differ.

**Calculation:** Count each penetration and measured flashing LF/area; price only required reset/repair/replacement.

**Evidence required:** Detail photos; penetration inventory; dimensions; applicable installation detail; reuse assessment.

**Likely objection:** Existing flashing is serviceable; pipe boot already includes flashing.

**Supported reply:** Demonstrate the missing specific detail or documented failed component.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Cosmetic change; replacing sound reusable components without reason.

**Overlap risk:** Boot with integral flashing plus same flashing separately; curb assembly overlap.

**Xactimate action:** Match operation and unit to each unique feature.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I10. **Sources:** A-S19, A-S21, A-S05.

## A-O11 Decking and localized structural substrate repair

**Potential discrepancy:** Concealed or observed deck repair omitted or over-assumed.

**Calculation:** Verified damaged/replacement area × matched thickness/material and necessary labor; show contingency separately.

**Evidence required:** Photos after exposure; thickness/span/fastening; moisture/rot distinction; qualified assessment; AHJ requirement if applicable.

**Likely objection:** No storm-related deck damage is established.

**Supported reply:** Provide location, extent, cause and repair-necessity evidence when exposed.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Blanket redeck; ordinary decay labelled storm damage; no code trigger.

**Overlap risk:** Deck in complete assembly and separate deck line; overlapping patches.

**Xactimate action:** Hold unverified area pending; add measured work with exposure photos.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I03, B-I08, B-I09. **Sources:** A-S21, A-S19, A-S26.

## A-O12 Ventilation components and roof-system ventilation

**Potential discrepancy:** Damaged vent replacement/reset or necessary integration omitted; unrelated redesign added.

**Calculation:** Count existing affected vents/LF; calculate any design change only under applicable documented requirement.

**Evidence required:** Vent inventory; photos; existing configuration; manufacturer detail; code trigger and qualified design if proposed.

**Likely objection:** Existing ventilation works; added ventilation is improvement.

**Supported reply:** Limit request to repair/restoration or specifically triggered compliance.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Heat/condensation maintenance issue alone; automatic whole-attic redesign.

**Overlap risk:** Ridge cap/vent bundled line plus duplicate separate components.

**Xactimate action:** Separate damaged component work from optional upgrade.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I10, B-I08, B-I09. **Sources:** A-S05, A-S18.

## A-O13 Drainage, scuppers, drains and low-slope transitions

**Potential discrepancy:** Damaged drainage or necessary tie-in omitted; pre-existing ponding correction included without support.

**Calculation:** Measured damaged features and necessary tie-in areas; compare reset versus replacement.

**Evidence required:** Water-path photos; drain/scupper dimensions; storm debris versus maintenance record; system details.

**Likely objection:** Blockage/ponding is maintenance or pre-existing design.

**Supported reply:** Show event-linked damage or required integration with supported repair.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Routine cleaning; unrelated slope redesign; no damage nexus.

**Overlap risk:** Drain/curb package and separate flashing; cleanup counted twice.

**Xactimate action:** Keep maintenance separate; note exact drainage feature and activity.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I03, B-I06. **Sources:** A-S21, A-S20, A-S26.

## A-O14 Access, height, steepness, staging and protection

**Potential discrepancy:** Documented site constraints not reflected or generic surcharges overstate them.

**Calculation:** Affected area or documented equipment/time × applicable rate, less included productivity/safety costs.

**Evidence required:** Slope/height measurements; access photos; staging plan; rental quote/duration; task-specific labor basis.

**Likely objection:** Normal setup/safety/productivity already included.

**Supported reply:** Identify incremental site-specific cost and exclusions in base items.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Standard job conditions; blanket unsafe-work surcharge; unsupported duration.

**Overlap risk:** High/steep lines plus custom labor for identical impediment.

**Xactimate action:** Check licensed item scope, trade and supporting-event assumptions before adding.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I12. **Sources:** A-S04, A-S06.

## A-O15 Solar, HVAC and other equipment detach/reset

**Potential discrepancy:** Necessary trade work to access roofing omitted, or replacement claimed where reset suffices.

**Calculation:** Equipment count × scoped detach/reset quote plus separately necessary testing/permit; remove duplicates.

**Evidence required:** Equipment layout; access interference; licensed trade proposal; owner/lease constraints; manufacturer reset protocol.

**Likely objection:** Equipment need not move; roofer quote includes it.

**Supported reply:** Show physical interference and least-cost compliant access method.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Optional relocation/upgrade; undamaged equipment replacement without necessity.

**Overlap risk:** Trade lump sum plus crane, electrical or reset labor already quoted.

**Xactimate action:** Use documented quote and precise scope; mark permit/commissioning pending.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I03, B-I12. **Sources:** A-S06, A-S17.

## A-O16 Debris hauling, disposal and conditional permits

**Potential discrepancy:** Required disposal/permit costs missing, already embedded or inapplicable.

**Calculation:** Measured disposal weight/volume and actual haul basis; applicable fee schedule/receipt; subtract included cleanup/disposal.

**Evidence required:** Removal item detail; disposal quote/tickets; site logistics; actual AHJ scope/fee confirmation.

**Likely objection:** Tear-off includes disposal; same-material Phoenix reroof is exempt.

**Supported reply:** Show net nonduplicative disposal and any specific permit trigger.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Automatic permit charge based only on the older Phoenix brochure; exempt work; unrelated debris; unsupported loads.

**Overlap risk:** Per-square tear-off disposal plus full dumpster charge; fee in trade quote.

**Xactimate action:** Inspect inclusions; separate justified job cost from conditional fee.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I14. **Sources:** A-S17, A-S21, A-S04.

## A-O17 Price-list date, geographic market and benchmark

**Potential discrepancy:** Estimates use different list periods, areas or modified baselines.

**Calculation:** Reprice unchanged supported scope on each recorded basis; isolate price delta from scope delta.

**Evidence required:** Exact list identifier/version; location/ZIP; estimate and repair dates; original checkpoint; basis rationale.

**Likely objection:** Carrier's list is appropriate to the adjustment date.

**Supported reply:** Explain factual basis for alternate market/date; provide both scenarios if unresolved.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Selecting most expensive period; silent repricing; unsupported catastrophe assumption.

**Overlap risk:** Date effect plus custom override for same price movement.

**Xactimate action:** Record Claim Info > Parameters; export Variation and preserve originals.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I12. **Sources:** A-S02, A-S13, A-S09, A-S25.

## A-O18 Documented local unit-price, labor and specialty overrides

**Potential discrepancy:** Published allowance differs from substantiated available job-specific cost.

**Calculation:** Quantity × evidenced rate difference, after matching scope, units, tax, delivery and markup.

**Evidence required:** Dated comparable supplier/trade bids; availability; detailed labor basis; quote exclusions.

**Likely objection:** Single high quote does not establish reasonable market cost.

**Supported reply:** Reconcile comparable scope and explain actual constraint or verified cost.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Unverifiable quote; target-driven markup; premium elective contractor preference.

**Overlap risk:** All-in quote with separate included labor/material/delivery/markup.

**Xactimate action:** Override transparently; attach quote and include Variation report.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I12. **Sources:** A-S01, A-S06, A-S09, A-S25.

## A-O19 Taxes, O&P, labor minimums and allocation

**Potential discrepancy:** Financial settings omit, duplicate or misapply a justified cost.

**Calculation:** Recalculate each eligible base once under verified jurisdiction/contract basis; show minimum shortfall separately.

**Evidence required:** Current tax authority determination; job/trade structure; actual supervisory need; scope-separated bids; minimum-group review.

**Likely objection:** Embedded trade overhead/minimums already cover cost; added general O&P unwarranted.

**Supported reply:** Separate embedded, job-specific and general costs with a job-specific basis.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Assumed fixed O&P rate; arbitrary trade-count rule; tax rate without classification.

**Overlap risk:** Markup on already marked-up quotes; minimum plus equivalent callout; tax duplication.

**Xactimate action:** Review Unit Price flags, Parameters and Labor minimums; retain assumptions.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I13, B-I14. **Sources:** A-S01, A-S06, A-S08.

## A-O20 RCV, ACV, depreciation, payments and final duplicate audit

**Potential discrepancy:** Gross scope difference confused with depreciation, deductible, prior payment or withheld item.

**Calculation:** Compare gross RCV first; reconcile documented depreciation and recoverability; calculate payment bridge separately under policy terms.

**Evidence required:** Full estimate summaries; line depreciation; roof age/condition records; payment ledger; policy/endorsements; coverage review.

**Likely objection:** Difference reflects policy settlement, not omitted repair cost.

**Supported reply:** Identify the exact accounting mismatch without asserting unsupported coverage.

**Withdraw or narrow when:** Treating contractor RCV as immediately payable; assuming all depreciation recoverable.

**Overlap risk:** Deductible/prior payment subtracted twice; repair and replacement overlap; allowance counted in multiple categories.

**Xactimate action:** Review item and project depreciation; reconcile totals and unique work IDs before export.

**Argument crosswalk:** B-I16, B-I17, B-I18. **Sources:** A-S14, A-S12, A-S15.
